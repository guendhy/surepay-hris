# Using Surepay HRIS — guide for HR, managers and employees

## Employees

**Sign in** with your work email. If your role handles sensitive data you will be asked to set up an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password) the first time.

**File leave** — *My leave → File leave*
1. Pick the leave type and dates. The box at the bottom shows how many days will be deducted (rest days and holidays are skipped), your available balance, and anything that blocks the request.
2. Choose half day (AM/PM) for single days where allowed.
3. Attach a document when asked (e.g. medical certificate for sick leave longer than 2 days).
4. Submit. Your supervisor is notified; you get a notification when it is approved or rejected.

**Cancel** a pending request, or an approved one that has not started, from *My leave*. Leave that has already started can only be cancelled by HR.

**Balances** — *My leave* shows each balance and a history of every credit and deduction. Vacation and sick leave accrue on the 1st of each month.

**Profile** — update your mobile number, personal email, address and emergency contact under *My profile*. For other corrections, send HR a request with supporting documents.

## Supervisors and department heads

- *Approvals* lists requests waiting for you. Tick one or more, add a comment (required to reject or return) and choose Approve, Return or Reject.
- Requests go to the immediate supervisor first, then the department head. If you are both, you only approve once.
- Requests not acted on within 2 working days are escalated to HR (you keep your task).
- Going on leave? *Delegation* sends new requests to a colleague for a date range.
- *Leave calendar* shows your team's approved and pending leave.

## HR

**Add an employee** — *Employees → Add employee*. The employee number (SPT-YYYY-0001) and regularisation due date are filled in automatically. Then on the employee page:
- *IDs & pay*: government IDs (stored encrypted; "Reveal" is logged) and compensation (every change needs a reason and goes into salary history).
- *Documents*: upload contracts, clearances, certificates; set expiry dates.
- *201 profile*: **Send invitation** so the employee can sign in.
- Changing department, position, supervisor, type or status is recorded automatically in *History*. Setting status to **Separated** disables the employee's login.

**Opening balances at go-live** — employee → *Leave* tab → *Adjust balance* → entry "Opening balance".

**Leave requests** — *Leave requests* lists all requests with filters. HR can cancel any request (with a reason); approved days are returned to the balance.

**Balances report** — *Leave balances*; *Export to Excel* (exports are logged).

**Settings**
- *Leave types*: credits, accrual, carry-over and cash-conversion caps, filing rules and eligibility. Changes apply to new requests and the next accrual run.
- *Holidays & schedules*: verify each holiday against the Proclamation; add Eid'l Fitr/Eid'l Adha when proclaimed; add local holidays per location; create schedules (e.g. compressed work week) and assign them.
- *Approval flows*: special routing for a leave type or department (e.g. maternity leave → HR Manager).
- *Organisation*: departments (set the department head — they approve the second step), positions, job levels, locations.

**Year-end** runs automatically on 1 January: unused leave is carried over up to the cap, then converted to cash up to the conversion cap, and the rest is forfeited. Review the conversion entries before running payroll.

## Administrators

- *Users & roles*: grant/revoke roles and disable accounts. Every account automatically has the Employee role. You cannot change your own roles.
- *Audit log*: who changed what and when, plus sign-ins, exports and views of sensitive data. Entries cannot be edited or deleted.
